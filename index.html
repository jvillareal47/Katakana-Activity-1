Welcome, Students!

Japanese uses Katakana to spell foreign words (Gairaigo). Because Japanese relies on a moraic structure (consonant-vowel syllables), English words are adapted to fit Japanese phonetics. For example, "Sandwich" becomes "Sandoitchi" (サンドイッチ) and "McDonald's" becomes "Makudonarudo" (マクドナルド).
